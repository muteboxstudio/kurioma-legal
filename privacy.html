Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Kurioma ("the app") is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Kurioma channel to upload the owner's own short videos to the owner's YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • TikTok: basic profile information (user.info.basic) to confirm the connected account, and permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload).
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos (youtube.upload).

How we use and store it

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are used solely to upload videos created by the owner. We do not collect data from other users, we do not sell or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Google account settings; deleting them from the computer removes all stored data.

Política de privacidad

Kurioma es una herramienta de escritorio privada que solo usa el dueño del canal Kurioma para subir sus propios videos a sus cuentas de YouTube y TikTok. Accede únicamente a la información básica del perfil y al permiso de subida. Los tokens se guardan solo en el ordenador del dueño, no se comparten ni se venden datos, y pueden revocarse en cualquier momento desde la configuración de TikTok o Google.
